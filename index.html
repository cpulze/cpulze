Why it matters

AI tools may already generate a picture of your hotel. That picture may be outdated, incomplete, or inconsistent with current operations.

Guests, booking assistants, and AI tools build that picture from reviews, listings, and third‑party mentions long before they reach your site. The problem is, it may rest on missing evidence, weak or stale signals, and facts you don't control, so some AI responses may present a version of your hotel that no longer matches current operations.

What is an AI footprint?
Your AI footprint is the publicly available material that may be retrieved, cited, or reflected when AI tools describe your property — including reviews across TripAdvisor, Booking.com and Google, editorial mentions, OTA listings, travel forums and comparison threads. cpulze analyses that footprint and identifies recurring themes observed across the tools and questions included in your scan, rather than relying on a single response on a single day.
What will I receive in a scan?
A reviewed, plain-language snapshot: the recurring themes found in selected AI responses to structured booking-stage questions, the relevant public source material behind them, and practical next steps worth considering. It's a focused diagnostic, not a complete audit of every AI tool, every online source, or every guest opinion — but it is designed to show you where to look first.
Do you contact review platforms or remove reviews?
No — and that's deliberate. cpulze doesn't remove, edit, suppress, or challenge reviews on TripAdvisor, Google, Booking.com, or anywhere else; if a review breaches a platform's rules, that's between you and the platform directly. What we do instead is show you how the public record is being read, and help you put current, hotel-approved information alongside it.
What is OwnerVoice?
OwnerVoice is your hotel-approved, date-stamped public record — a clear statement of completed changes, current arrangements, or facts you want stated plainly, sitting alongside the reviews and listings AI tools already draw on. You review and approve everything before it goes live, helping ensure it is accurate and current at the time of publication.
How does OwnerVoice help shape what AI says about my hotel?
Publishing accurate, owner-verified information creates a clearer, current public record that may be available to search engines and AI tools. Some hotels also choose to link to their OwnerVoice page from their own website, where appropriate. cpulze cannot promise whether, when, or how a particular AI tool or search engine will retrieve, cite, display, or reflect any update — that depends on the third-party platform, its systems, and the information available at the time. Where monitoring is included, we repeat comparable checks and report material changes observed in those checks.
Which AI engines do you scan?
We currently scan selected AI tools, including Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google Gemini, subject to the agreed scope and tool availability. Different tools retrieve and present information differently, which is why we report findings separately per engine rather than as one blended answer — so you can see where the issue appears most clearly in the responses tested.
How often do AI responses change?
Not on a fixed schedule — which is exactly why staying current matters. Older reviews and references can sit in the public record for years after a hotel has changed, so a structured scan (and where included, ongoing monitoring that repeats comparable checks) helps you understand what was observed in the responses tested at that time, instead of waiting to notice.
Is this the same as SEO or review management?
No. SEO is about where you rank in Google search results; review management improves your star rating for guests who read reviews directly. cpulze addresses a newer, separate moment: what AI tools say when a guest asks a direct question and may rely on an AI-generated response before visiting your website, a review platform, or another booking channel. That's the moment cpulze is built for.
Does cpulze work for my type of property?
Yes — cpulze is built for independent and boutique hotels of any size, from a single property to a multi-property portfolio. Smaller properties may have fewer public reviews and references available, which can make a small number of recurring or specific comments more noticeable in the responses tested; larger properties and groups get value from seeing patterns and competitive positioning at scale. cpulze is designed for properties that want a clearer view of how selected AI tools respond to booking-stage questions.
What do you need from the hotel?
To get started, just your hotel name, location, website, and an authorised contact — that's it for the initial scan. If you move forward with a deeper review or an OwnerVoice update, we'll ask you to confirm the relevant facts and dates, with sign-off from an authorised hotel representative before anything is published.
Is my information kept confidential?
Non-public information you share with us is used to deliver the agreed service and handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. OwnerVoice content is published only after approval by an authorised hotel representative.
