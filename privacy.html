Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  ·  Data controller: Nuwayz Systems Ltd, trading as cpulze

Summary

  • We collect only what's needed to run AI narrative scans, deliver reports, and manage your hotel's account with us.
  • We never sell personal data, use advertising cookies, or build guest profiles.
  • AI tools (Perplexity, Anthropic/Claude) help structure findings, but nothing is published without human review and hotel confirmation.
  • Object to marketing anytime by replying "unsubscribe" or "no thanks" or emailing privacy@cpulze.com — we'll stop immediately.

1. What we collect

  • Website visitors: IP address, device/browser info, pages visited, security logs.
  • Scan requests: name, work email, hotel name/location/website, your stated objectives.
  • Paid clients: the above, plus billing details, scan/report history, action-tracker data, OwnerVoice drafts and approvals, and documents you submit for publication.
  • Business contacts (before you reach out to us): name, role, work email, hotel/company name — sourced from public business listings, directories, and professional networks.
  • Public guest reviews: reviewed to inform scans; we don't identify anonymous reviewers or use reviews for marketing.

We don't collect payment-card details directly (handled by Stripe), and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

2. Why we use it

To run scans and deliver reports; manage billing, support, and renewals; operate OwnerVoice (drafting, approval, publishing, corrections); secure our systems; and meet legal/tax obligations. We may also contact relevant hotel decision-makers with information about cpulze — you can opt out anytime.

Legal bases: contract performance (delivering services you've requested), legitimate interests (security, service improvement, relevant B2B outreach), and legal obligation (tax/accounting records). Marketing is always subject to your absolute right to object.

3. AI and automation

AI tools help structure findings and compare text against public feedback. We never use fully automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals, and nothing is published as fact without review — findings are routed for hotel confirmation first.

4. OwnerVoice

Publishes hotel-approved, date-stamped facts about your property. You're responsible for confirming accuracy before publication. Pages may be indexed by search engines or AI tools — we can't control third-party crawling once content is public. Flag inaccuracies: corrections@cpulze.com.

6. Who we share it with

Service providers only, each bound to use data solely to deliver their service: Perplexity AI and Anthropic/Claude (scan processing), Stripe (payments), Supabase (data storage), Vercel (hosting), Cloudflare (security), Brevo (email delivery), Google Workspace (document sharing), plus advisers and authorities where legally required.

We never sell data or share one hotel's confidential material with another.

7. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK/EEA. Where this happens, we use approved safeguards (UK IDTA, SCC Addendum, or adequacy regulations). Details on request: privacy@cpulze.com.

8. Security

HTTPS encryption, access controls, MFA where available, encrypted secrets, and rate limiting/bot protection. No system is fully secure — we'll notify affected individuals of any breach as required by law.

9. Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection (including an absolute right to object to marketing), and the right to complain to the ICO. Contact privacy@cpulze.com — we respond within one month.
